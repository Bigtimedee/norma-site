NORMA

Sports alerts and wager tracking.

NORMA is a mobile application for sports fans who want to keep track of the games that matter to them. Follow your teams, receive alerts when games start and when something changes, and keep a personal record of wagers you have placed elsewhere.

What NORMA does

  • Game alerts: personalized notifications for the teams you follow.
  • Wager tracking: a private record of your own bets, entered by you, for your own reference.
  • Live odds: odds displayed from major sportsbooks for information only.
  • Prediction markets: optional connections to prediction market accounts you already hold.

What NORMA is not

NORMA does not facilitate, process, or broker any wagers or bets. It is not a sportsbook, betting exchange, or gambling operator, and it does not provide gambling advice or betting recommendations. All information in the app is provided for informational purposes only. NORMA is intended for users 18 years of age or older.
